1. What process currently requires two or more approvals or repeated manual handoffs?

### Objection: Why not use Zapier?

For simple notification or routing automations, Zapier or native features may be a better fit. RivetFlow is only useful where you need explicit approval decisions, run versioning, controlled state transitions and an inspectable audit. I would not propose this if a basic no-code recipe solves your need.

### Objection: Why would we pay for an MIT project?

You would not be buying ownership of open-source code. You would be purchasing the design, customization, integration, validation and handover necessary to solve your specific workflow.

### Objection: Is this production-ready?

The public code includes production-deployed elements, but it is not an audited enterprise SaaS. Security, identity and operational requirements are reviewed before we accept an engagement. If your requirements exceed the platform's controls, the correct answer is to decline or scope additional work.
